About Supraja

Supraja is a fertilizer supply company serving farmers, retailers and agricultural businesses. We source and supply the nutrients crops need, and we help customers choose the right product for their soil and season.

Our goal is simple: dependable quality, fair dealing and supply that arrives on time.
